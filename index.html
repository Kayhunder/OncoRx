Cancer Treatment

Cancer Treatment & Care

Explore common cancer treatment approaches and the role each can play in a patient's overall care plan.

Surgery

Surgical treatment may be used to remove tumors, obtain tissue for diagnosis or manage complications.

Radiation Therapy

Radiation uses carefully planned energy to treat cancer cells and may be used alone or alongside other treatments.

Systemic Therapy

Systemic treatments include chemotherapy, targeted medicines, immunotherapy and other medicines that act throughout the body.

Supportive Care

Supportive care helps manage symptoms, treatment side effects, nutrition, emotional wellbeing and quality of life.
