Targeted Oncology Agents

Precision therapies aimed at molecular cancer drivers.

Targeted oncology agents are designed to inhibit specific proteins or pathways that fuel tumor growth. These therapies often include kinase inhibitors, PARP inhibitors, monoclonal antibodies, and hormone receptor modulators. OncoRx highlights dosing nuances, resistance patterns, and combination strategies that support safer, more effective therapy choices.

Key clinical considerations include biomarker selection, patient eligibility, toxicity monitoring, and drug-drug interactions with supportive oncology regimens.

Immunotherapy & Biologics

Immune-based treatments shaping modern cancer care.

Immunotherapies harness the body's immune system to recognize and destroy cancer cells. OncoRx covers checkpoint inhibitors, cellular therapies, and antibody-drug conjugates, emphasizing safety management, response assessment, and adverse event mitigation.

These therapies often require careful multidisciplinary coordination, including patient selection, immune-related toxicity monitoring, and supportive care to preserve quality of life during treatment.

Supportive Care & Biosimilars

Improving tolerability, adherence, and care efficiency.

Supportive care agents and biosimilars play a vital role in oncology by managing side effects, supporting nutrition, and preserving immune function. OncoRx explains how these therapies complement systemic cancer treatments and reduce the overall burden of care.

Best practices for supportive care include proactive symptom control, treatment continuity planning, and selecting biosimilar options that maintain efficacy while improving access and affordability.
